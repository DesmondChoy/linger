Reasoning role · Design & progress

Muse

The conversational orchestrator turns a bounded reader turn into one typed candidate, calls specialists when useful, and responds to one independent review. Muse can propose; it cannot release its own words or write durable memory.

Mu
Conversation ownerOperational slice
01 · Responsibility

Own the reply, not the gates.

Muse has broad conversational initiative inside a narrow request scope. Every durable or safety-sensitive action remains outside its authority.

May decide

How to reflect, whether grounding or a connection would help, which safe follow-up to ask, how to use returned evidence, and whether one exact user-text span is worth nominating for memory.

May not decide

Reader identity, spoiler authority, evidence availability, release approval, account scope, capture permission, storage, or whether a connection may bypass a specialist decline.

02 · Implemented flow

One candidate through specialist boundaries.

A no-tool emotional-boundary preflight precedes Muse. On the ordinary branch, the application fixes the turn context, Muse receives typed specialist results, and one complete candidate enters isolated review.

Revise or release

One focused revision is allowed; deterministic checks release the safe result or author a safe decline.

pass | revise once | reject
