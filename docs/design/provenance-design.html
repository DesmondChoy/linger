Provenance

The isolated safety role runs a no-tool emotional-boundary preflight on the current Line, then reviews every complete Muse candidate as untrusted data. It decides whether a response may ship and, separately, whether a proposed memory may be captured—without storage authority.

Pr
Independent release gateMandatory gate
01 · Responsibility

Classify the boundary, then review the candidate.

Provenance is isolated so Muse cannot approve its own wording, evidence claims, spoiler scope, emotional-policy compliance, or automatic memory nomination.

May decide

Continue ordinary reflection or require the fixed emotional boundary; pass, revise, or reject a candidate response; issue response- or capture-scoped findings; allow, reject, or record no automatic memory candidate; and give one focused response critique for revision.

May not decide

What Muse should say initially, which evidence to retrieve, who the account is, what storage policy permits, whether a rejected draft may be shown, or whether an application-owned response may write memory. It has no tools or direct memory access.

02 · Implemented flow

Preflight, then two scoped verdicts.

The preflight sees only the current Line and emotional policy. Candidate review receives a separate strict envelope assembled by the application. Missing evidence, invalid findings, and unclear boundaries fail closed.

04 · Verification

Contract coverage, a focused safety pack, and replay traces.

Deterministic review and orchestration tests cover the full release contract. A versioned eight-case suite measures emotional-boundary misses and over-refusal without storing evaluated Lines or rationales.

Focused testsSuite

Preflight, review envelopes, finding locations, response and capture decisions, revision, deterministic release, suppression, and rejected-draft leak prevention.

Fixed eval cases8

Distress, inability to cope, ordinary frustration, literary or quoted language, concern for another person, and embedded instructions.

Synthetic replay11

The capture package records Provenance preflight and review hand-offs with matching Logfire trace IDs; no dedicated provider-backed classifier report is checked in.
