Reasoning role · Design & progress

Serendipity

The connection specialist proposes one tentative, evidence-backed resonance—or declines safely. Application orchestration fixes its book, chapter, and source scope before dispatch, and the specialist itself remains pure: no tools, network, storage, or account authority.

Se
Connection specialistBook-only slice
01 · Responsibility

Discover inside a fixed scope.

Serendipity interprets an already-authorised evidence bundle. It cannot widen the search or work around missing support.

May decide

Whether the supplied cue and evidence support a tentative connection, its uncertainty, the evidence IDs that back it, and a safe follow-up—or whether to decline for insufficient evidence or unsupported scope.

May not decide

When it is called, the reader’s book or chapter ceiling, allowed source kinds, what Librarian retrieves, whether private memory may leave its boundary, or whether Muse may invent a connection after a decline.

02 · Implemented flow

Scope first, interpretation last.

Muse supplies only a cue. Application-owned orchestration resolves the confirmed Alice scope and hands retrieval to Librarian before pure proposal logic runs.

03 · Privacy guard

Fail closed

Retrieve evidence

05 · Serendipity

Propose or decline
