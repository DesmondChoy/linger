## Priorities and limits

1. Prevent ambiguous matches from becoming authority; this caused actual releases in three books.
2. Remove model re-encoding of an application-owned inclusive scope; this hid valid evidence in two cases.
3. Retain alternative event candidates privately and resolve reranker token handling without converting relevance into permission.
4. Make valid uncertainty outputs easier to produce and preserve safe failure handling.

The checks above establish causes for these saved runs and reproduce specific structural/ranking behavior locally. They do not establish live success after a proposed fix. Exact support-set grading and prose-completeness gaps are documented in the per-scenario analyses and remain separate from these runtime defects. All four distinctive inference failures limited to support-set matching still found their stopping events; Alice’s ambiguity Scene correctly clarified. Those controls argue against describing the entire decomposition change as a retrieval failure.
