## Identity-theme needs a scoped expectation decision

The query asks how changing size and uncertain identity reinforce one another through Chapter 5. Gold expects weak and ranges Chapter 4:762–770 and Chapter 5:966–981. Both runs select the explicit Caterpillar identity exchange and a later height/mushroom passage; release also selects the size/memory passage. These are real, relevant alternatives. Missing the Chapter 4 range explains recall 0.5; extra Chapter 5 records explain precision 0.5 direct and 1/3 release. This is not fabricated evidence or a scope violation. See [case definition](/Users/kevinmanuel/Documents/REPO/linger/evals/librarian/cases.json:13).

There is also a substantive strength-calibration question. The release reviewer rejects the first draft’s reverse causal assertion—that identity uncertainty makes each new size feel like proof of transformation—and the claim that Alice requires a particular size to feel normal. The revision appropriately states the narrower bodily-change/identity link and her discomfort at three inches. Thus useful support exists, but the original “reinforce each other” premise is not established as a literal two-way causal mechanism. Sufficient may be reasonable for a bounded literary interpretation; weak may be reasonable if full reciprocal causation is required. The saved case contains no separate rationale settling that distinction. Preserve the grades and decide the intended question and admissible alternative passages together, rather than automatically treating either the model or fixed gold as correct.
