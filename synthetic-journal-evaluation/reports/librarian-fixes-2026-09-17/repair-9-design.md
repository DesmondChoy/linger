## Restore ordinary reflective exploration

One review asks for tentative alternatives and the next rejects those alternatives under an overly broad personal-causation rule. The product specification permits non-factual reflection; the adopted proposal asks for this exploration without retrieval. Replace the blanket rule with a distinction between reader-requested nonclinical possibilities grounded in their stated details and claims that research establishes an individual's cause. Preserve unknown history, diagnosis, sensitive-trait and source attribution boundaries. Keep a reader's later explanation distinct from facts about their earlier motives. This changes role instructions, not ground truth or release authority.

## Chosen support contract

Independent review agreed to the small coherent change rather than reordering alone: ClaimSupportAudit emits group_index, source_contributions, required support_summary (nonblank, at most800 characters), then supported. ProvenanceReview emits coverage/quotation/claim audits, prior finding resolutions, findings and final decisions. The summary is a concise evidence conclusion, not private reasoning or an independent proof. No semantic regex or model-call stage is added. Existing current-input validators remain authoritative for inventory, source-local excerpt binding and grounded negative findings. All owned fixture callers migrate to the required field; saved evaluation exchange outputs remain unmodified historical data. The candidate-review instruction is changed in place to match the new order.
