# Stage 2 chapter-cue proposal: The Adventures of Pinocchio

Word budget: 60 per chapter. Approve with `uv run python -m evals.librarian.chapter_cue_recall approve --stage 2`.

## Failure patterns Sculptor named

- Chapter 11’s current aids do not sufficiently cue the distinct turning point: Fire Eater’s pity is shown by sneezing, and Pinocchio’s offer to take Harlequin’s place wins the pardon. The question was a miss despite nearby theater chapters being returned.
- Chapter 12’s current description makes it too easy to confuse the first encounter with Fox and Cat with their later reunion in Chapter 18. Distinguish the first meeting by the five coins Fire Eater gives for Geppetto and the Fox’s and Cat’s invitation to the Field of Wonders.
- Chapter 18 was returned for the buried-coins question, but search did not reach the passage. This is a passage-retrieval miss, not a chapter-selection failure, so chapter aids cannot repair it.
